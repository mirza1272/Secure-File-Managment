What happens when you register?
  • A new user account is created with secure password hashing (SHA-256)
  • An X.509 digital certificate is generated for you
  • Your certificate is signed by our Certificate Authority (CA)
  • A public/private key pair (RSA-2048) is created for you
  • You can use this to digitally sign your encrypted files
